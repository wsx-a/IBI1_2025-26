The human DLX5 protein (UniProt ID: P56178) is 289 amino acids long and is located in the nucleus.
I ran BLAST against Eutheria (placental mammals) and obtained 250 hits. The percentage identity ranged from 52.3% to 100%. The mouse DLX5 (P70396) showed 96.5% identity with human DLX5.
I then used Python to do a non-gapped global alignment with the BLOSUM62 matrix, which is a standard scoring matrix for protein sequence alignment. I compared three pairs:
Human vs Mouse: Score = 1452, Identity = 96.54%
Human vs Random: Score = -1110, Identity = 1.73%
Mouse vs Random: Score = -1110, Identity = 1.73%
Human and mouse DLX5 have much higher scores and identity than random sequences. This means they are the most closely related and highly conserved.
